Purpose and framing

This catalogue consolidates the public articles, regulator publications, official case releases and research reports identified during the insider-threat discussion. It is designed to support development of an Insider Access Exposure Score: a measure of harm possible from access and control gaps, not an assessment of an employee's morality or intent.

Important: Worker location, citizenship, nationality or ethnicity should not be treated as an individual integrity score. Cross-border and third-party work arrangements may be assessed only through measurable control conditions such as data location, monitoring, subcontractor visibility, revocation speed and regulatory access.

Risk personas supported by the evidence

Privileged technology operator

System, network, database, UNIX, cloud or production-development staff able to change or disrupt critical platforms.

Primary risk: sabotage, outage, destructive changes, log tampering.

Sensitive client-data custodian

Staff with customer-data, reporting extracts, shared-data folders or bulk export capability.

Primary risk: mass privacy breach and identity fraud.

Transaction / control operator

Staff able to open accounts, issue cards, unblock restrictions, process payments or override controls.

Primary risk: fraud, laundering and control bypass.

Worker-arrangement modifiers

ArrangementEvidence-based treatment
Contractor / consultantRaise exposure only where critical access, oversight gaps or offboarding limitations exist.
Vendor / subcontractorAssess bank visibility, audit rights, endpoint controls, data access and revocation authority.
Offshore / cross-border deliveryAssess the control environment and data-handling arrangement, not perceived morality by country.
Intern / co-op / work-studyRaise exposure where production/client data is unnecessary for training or supervision is weak.
Part-time / temporaryScore the transaction or data authority, not hours worked.

Classification corrections and cautions

  • Desjardins is verified as an employee data-exfiltration case, but public findings do not establish that the employee was a system administrator.
  • Capital One / Paige Thompson is an external technology-ecosystem case involving a former cloud-company employee, not a Capital One employee-insider case.
  • Terry Childs is a strong network-administrator operational-disruption comparison, but it involved the City of San Francisco, not a bank.
  • Cases reported at charge or allegation stage are labelled accordingly; they should not be described as convictions unless the listed status confirms it.
